Flagship convening property

College Esports Expo (CEX)

CEX has earned lasting recognition across the collegiate esports industry by bringing together the people shaping the market—educators, administrators, students, operators, brands, publishers, technology leaders and innovators—to address the questions that matter most.

A respected industry forum

Built from the classroom. Proven in the market.

College Esports Expo began as an extension of classroom discussions and think-tank conversations about how higher education should respond to the rapid growth of competitive gaming. It evolved into a large-scale forum connecting esports with academics, careers, student life, media, technology, wellness, business and professional opportunity.

By 2020, CEX had grown to 300 attendees, 53 speakers and 38 panels across five programming tracks—evidence of the demand for a dedicated place where the collegiate esports community could meet, learn, debate and build relationships.

Across later editions, CEX continued to convene multi-campus teams, industry executives, educators and practitioners around themes such as Innovation, Monetization, Wellness and Fellowship. Even through a hosting hiatus, the event has retained strong recognition and goodwill across the industry.
