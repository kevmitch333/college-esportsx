About College EsportsX

College esports is bigger than the gaming room.

College EsportsX is a strategic advisory and market-development platform for the collegiate esports ecosystem. We connect competition with academics, student life, workforce development, facilities, technology, sponsorship, media and community so institutions and partners can build programs with a clearer purpose and stronger outcomes.

How we work

From ambiguity to an executable roadmap.

Our process keeps strategy practical. We diagnose the current state, define the opportunity, design the operating model and create a prioritized path to implementation.

01 — Assess

Stakeholders, demand, assets, constraints, competitive environment and institutional goals.

02 — Design

Program architecture, offerings, partnerships, facilities, curriculum and measurement.

03 — Activate

Pilots, events, partner outreach, student engagement and launch planning.

04 — Scale

Refine what works, build recurring revenue or funding paths and expand participation.
