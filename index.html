Built from the market

From classroom conversations to an industry platform.

The College Esports Expo grew from classroom discussion and think-tank style conversations into a recurring forum where educators, operators, students, brands and esports leaders could tackle the practical issues shaping collegiate gaming.

Academic roots

Created from the need to help colleges understand a fast-changing competitive gaming ecosystem.

Industry convening

Panels and workshops connected esports with academics, careers, media, technology and professional sports.

Applied programming

Sessions addressed facilities, coaching, curriculum, sustainability, PR, platforms, wellness and student leadership.

Flagship convening platform

College Esports Expo (CEX)

CEX is where the people building college esports come together to compare what is working, surface hard problems, discover new technology, share research and create partnerships.

Future editions can function as both a conference and a market laboratory—bringing institutions, students and industry partners into demonstrations, workshops, competitions and collaborative working sessions.
