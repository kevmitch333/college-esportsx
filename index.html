Flagship convening property

College Esports Expo (CEX)

CEX is a respected collegiate esports platform that has brought together educators, administrators, students, operators, brands, technology leaders, military esports teams and innovators around the future of the college esports ecosystem.

A respected industry forum

More than an event. A collegiate esports institution.

College Esports Expo grew out of classroom discussion and industry dialogue about how higher education should respond to competitive gaming. It developed into a forum connecting esports with academics, careers, student life, media, technology, wellness, business and professional opportunity.

CEX has operated across live events, remote programming, special-event formats, international markets, education, military esports and industry partnerships. Its flexibility is part of the value of the platform: CEX is not defined by one venue or one format.

At its Boston scale, CEX reached 300 attendees, 53 speakers and 38 panels across five programming tracks. The brand has also extended internationally, including CEX Brazil, with CEX licensed in Brazil, India, South Africa and Indonesia.
